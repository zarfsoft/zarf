Zarf: Privacy policy

Zarfsoft. The same words as the Privacy policy screen in the app, under Settings.

Zarf collects nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, and no analytics or crash reporting of any kind.

Your progress (the words and rules you learn, every answer you give, the lessons you finish, the verses you read, your bookmark and your settings) is kept in the app’s own storage on this phone. It never leaves the phone unless you export it yourself with Export progress, and then it goes only where you choose to save or send it.

Zarf uses the internet for three things only, all in the Play Store build: downloading surah audio from Google Play, checking for an update through Google Play when you tap Check for updates, and taking a donation through Google Play when you choose to give. Google Play delivers the audio and handles the update and the payment under its own privacy policy. The Quran text, the meanings and everything else ship inside the app. The owner’s own build does none of these and uses no network at all.

Zarf is free for all, with nothing locked. A donation is made in Google Play’s own payment sheet: Zarf never sees your card or your Google account details and keeps no record of who gives. Google Play handles the payment under its own privacy policy.

Zarf asks Android only for these permissions: internet access, for those uses; the right to see whether the phone is online, so a waiting download can say it is waiting for the network; the right to download audio without showing a notification; Google Play billing (in the Play Store build), so a donation can be made; the right to keep a recitation playing when you leave the app (Android shows a notice while it plays) and to keep the phone awake until that recitation stops; in the Play Store build, the right to keep a surah download going in the background and to carry on a waiting download after the phone restarts, both used by Google Play’s download library; and your approximate location. Only the location asks you anything, and only when you tap Use my location in Prayer times: it is read once, rounded, kept in the app’s settings on this phone to work out the times, and never sent anywhere. You can pick a city instead and never be asked. Zarf does not ask for your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Uninstalling Zarf removes everything it keeps on the phone. A progress file you exported stays where you saved it.
